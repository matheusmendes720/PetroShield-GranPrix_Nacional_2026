# 2. ANPD ENFORCEMENT ACTIONS - REGULATORY LANDSCAPE

## 📋 RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

### Lei 15.352/2026 - ANPD Becomes Regulatory Agency
- **Status:** ANPD now "Autarquia Especial" (Special Autarchy)
- **Effect:** Enhanced enforcement powers
- **Implication:** Stricter oversight of data protection compliance

### Current Enforcement Priorities (2026)

#### 1. Age Verification Law (ECA Digital)
- **Effective:** March 17, 2026
- **Target:** Adult content websites
- **Risk:** Fines up to R$ 50 million
- **Relevance:** Shows ANPD is actively enforcing across sectors

#### 2. International Data Transfers
- **Resolution 19/2024:** Now fully operational
- **Requirement:** Brazil-specific SCCs (not EU SCCs)
- **Risk:** Non-compliance = up to 2% of Brazilian revenue

### ANPD 2026-2027 Regulatory Agenda
**Priority Topics:**
1. Artificial Intelligence governance
2. Children's data protection (ECA Digital implementation)
3. Binding Corporate Rules (BCR) procedures
4. Cross-border data flows

**Implication for Petrobras:**
- AI projects (Libra Rocks) need RIPD (Data Protection Impact Assessment)
- Vendor ecosystem must comply with international transfer rules
- Incident reporting: 3 business days to ANPD
