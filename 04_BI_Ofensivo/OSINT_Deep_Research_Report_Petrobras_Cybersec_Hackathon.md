## 2.3 Recent Cybersecurity Breach - CRITICAL INTELLIGENCE

### The Everest Ransomware Attack (November 2025)

**Incident Summary:**
- **Date:** November 14-17, 2025
- **Attacker:** Everest Ransomware Group
- **Vector:** Compromised credentials of contractor **SA Exploration**
- **Data Exfiltrated:** 90+ GB of Petrobras confidential data + 176 GB of SA Exploration data

**Exposed Data Categories:**
- Raw seismic navigation data (3D/4D seismic research)
- Ship coordinates and DGPS positioning data
- Ocean Bottom Node (OBN) positions
- Quality control parameters for survey lines
- Equipment specifications and configurations
- Firing pressures and hydrophone depths
- **CRITICAL:** Campos Basin seismic research data (announced same day as new oil discovery)

**Attack Significance:**
- Exposed **supply chain vulnerability** - attacker entered through contractor systems
- Demonstrated **credential stuffing** effectiveness
- Highlighted **third-party vendor risk**
- Data could enable competitors to replicate exploration strategies
- Timing coincided with major oil discovery announcement (possible strategic timing)

**Petrobras Response:**
- Confirmed "unauthorized exposure of information" via service provider
- Denied direct internal system breach
- Reinforced security guidelines with contractors
- Initiated monitoring and investigation

## 2.4 Why Petrobras Needs Cybersecurity Innovation (The "WHY")

### Immediate Drivers:

1. **Supply Chain Vulnerability Crisis**
   - SA Exploration breach proves third-party vendors are weakest link
   - Complex ecosystem of thousands of contractors/suppliers
   - Need for: Supply chain security monitoring, vendor risk assessment tools

2. **Critical Infrastructure Protection**
   - Operates 80+ offshore platforms
   - Pre-salt operations are national strategic assets
   - OT/ICS systems increasingly interconnected
   - Need for: OT security, SCADA protection, network segmentation

3. **Regulatory & Compliance Pressure**
   - LGPD (Brazilian GDPR) enforcement intensifying
   - ANP (National Petroleum Agency) cybersecurity requirements
   - Potential CFIUS-style foreign investment scrutiny
   - Need for: Compliance automation, data governance, privacy tech

4. **Geopolitical Risk Exposure**
   - State-controlled company = high-value nation-state target
   - Pre-salt technology is strategic national asset
   - Energy sector increasingly targeted by APT groups
   - Need for: Threat intelligence, APT detection, zero-trust architecture

5. **Digital Transformation Acceleration**
   - $109B investment plan requires secure digital infrastructure
   - AI/ML adoption creates new attack surfaces
   - Remote operations and digital twins expand vulnerability
   - Need for: Secure AI/ML pipelines, cloud security, edge security

# PART 3: OIL & GAS CYBERSECURITY THREAT LANDSCAPE

## 3.1 Industry-Wide Threat Intelligence

### Global Trends (2025-2026):

1. **Ransomware Epidemic**
   - Energy sector #1 target for ransomware groups
   - Average downtime: 20+ days
   - Average ransom demand: $5M+

2. **OT/ICS Targeting Escalation**
   - CISA warnings on SCADA/ICS attacks
   - Dragos Q3/Q4 2025 reports show 40%+ increase in industrial ransomware
   - Attackers moving from IT to OT networks

3. **Supply Chain Attacks**
   - 60%+ of breaches originate from third parties
   - Vendors with VPN access = prime targets
   - Software supply chain compromises (SolarWinds-style)

4. **State-Sponsored Activity**
   - Energy infrastructure = critical national security target
   - Geopolitical tensions driving cyber espionage
   - Pre-positioning for potential conflict scenarios

## 3.2 Specific Vulnerabilities in Oil & Gas

| System Type | Vulnerability | Risk Level |
|-------------|--------------|------------|
| **SCADA/ICS** | Legacy systems, unpatched vulnerabilities | CRITICAL |
| **Remote Access** | VPN concentrators, RDP exposure | HIGH |
| **Cloud Migration** | Misconfigurations, identity issues | HIGH |
| **IoT Sensors** | Weak authentication, no encryption | MEDIUM |
| **Vendor Networks** | Trusted connections, poor segmentation | CRITICAL |
| **Email/Phishing** | Human factor, credential theft | HIGH |

## 3.3 Recent Major Energy Sector Incidents

1. **Colonial Pipeline (2021)** - $4.4M ransom, fuel shortages
2. **Norsk Hydro (2019)** - $70M+ losses, LockerGoga ransomware
3. **Saudi Aramco (2022)** - Shamoon wiper attack
4. **Poland Energy (Dec 2025)** - DynoWiper malware, 30+ wind/solar farms
5. **Petrobras (Nov 2025)** - 90GB data breach, Everest ransomware
